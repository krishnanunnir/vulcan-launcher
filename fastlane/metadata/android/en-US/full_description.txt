<p>Make your Android home screen your own with Vulcan: a customizable launcher with no ads, trackers, analytics or internet permission.</p>

<p>Arrange apps and widgets, organize your drawer, and choose a look that fits your wallpaper. Vulcan works entirely on your device and needs no account.</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Your layout:</b> home grids up to 12 by 12, up to 9 pages, subgrid positioning, resizable widgets and a dock you can hide or scroll. Lock your layout to prevent accidental edits.</li>
<li><b>Your apps, organized:</b> an alphabetical drawer with search, an optional letter index, tabs, folders and hidden apps.</li>
<li><b>Useful search:</b> find apps with typo-tolerant matching or initials, app shortcuts and Android settings. Calculate sums and hand timers and alarms to your clock app.</li>
<li><b>Gestures:</b> swipe, double-tap or pinch to open apps, notifications, the drawer and other actions. Give individual app icons a swipe-up action.</li>
<li><b>Your style:</b> icon packs, themed icons on Android 13+, icon shapes, custom labels and icons, folder styles, colours and scheduled night mode.</li>
<li><b>Now page:</b> optional music controls, upcoming calendar events and favourite contacts.</li>
<li><b>Bring your layout:</b> import apps, folders, dock and grid settings from a Nova .novabackup file. Widgets need to be added again.</li>
<li><b>Local backups:</b> export and restore your setup using a readable JSON file.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Privacy and optional access</b><br>Vulcan has no internet permission or tracking SDKs. Contacts, calendar and approximate location are optional and used locally for the features you enable. Notification access is optional for badges, previews and media controls. Backups go to the destination you choose. External apps handle web and voice search when requested.</p>

<p><b>Accessibility service</b><br>Vulcan uses the AccessibilityService API only for system actions you trigger through gestures or shortcuts: locking the screen, recent apps, notifications, quick settings and the power menu. It cannot retrieve screen content and does not record interactions or collect or share personal data. This access is optional and can be disabled in Android settings.</p>

<p>Requires Android 8 or newer. Choose Vulcan in Android Settings &gt; Apps &gt; Default apps &gt; Home app. Switch back to another launcher at any time.</p>
